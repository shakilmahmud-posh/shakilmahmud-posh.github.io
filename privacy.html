Privacy Policy

Offline Archive · effective 22 September 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers Offline Archive, a command-line tool that copies openly published AI models into storage you control. It is published by Shakil Mahmud.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Files the app creates in your Google Drive. The app requests only the drive.file permission. It creates folders and files for your archive and reads their names, sizes and checksums to verify uploads and resume unfinished transfers. It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.
  • The email address of the signed-in account. The app reads it once per run to confirm you signed in with the account you intended.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, calendars, photos or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Only to carry out the transfers you start. Nothing else.

Where the data is stored

Your sign-in token and the app's transfer records are stored only on the computer or server where you run the app. The developer operates no server and receives no data, logs, usage statistics or analytics.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. There is no advertising and no tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Offline Archive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the app's token file on your machine to remove the stored sign-in.
  • Files the app created in your Drive are yours. Delete them in Google Drive whenever you like.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
