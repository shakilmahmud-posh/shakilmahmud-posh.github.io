Offline Archive

Keep your own verified copy of openly published AI models, and the software to run them.

What it does

Offline Archive is a command-line tool. It copies AI model files and runtimes from their official sources (Hugging Face and GitHub releases) into storage you control: your own Google Drive or a local disk.

  • Every file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 hash before it counts as done.
  • Transfers resume from the exact byte where they stopped, after a crash, a reboot or a lost connection.
  • Nothing is staged on your computer's disk when the destination is Google Drive.

How it uses Google Drive

It asks for one permission, drive.file. That lets it see and change only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot open anything else in your Drive.

Where it runs

On your own computer or server. Your Google sign-in is stored on that machine and nowhere else. The developer runs no server and receives no data from you.
